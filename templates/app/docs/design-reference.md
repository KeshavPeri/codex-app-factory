# Design reference

## Personality

Three to five adjectives describing how the product should feel.

## References

For each reference, name the specific quality to borrow. Do not ask the builder to copy an entire product.

## Typography and colour

Record deliberate choices or constraints from the design workshop.

## Interaction principles

State the most important mobile, accessibility, feedback, motion, and error-handling expectations.

## Avoid

List visual clichés or patterns that would make the product feel generic or inappropriate.
