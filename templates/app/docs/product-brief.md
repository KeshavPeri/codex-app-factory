# Product brief

## Outcome and deadline

What must be usable, by whom, and by when?

## Users and primary journey

Who uses the app, and what is the shortest successful journey?

## V1 features

List only the capabilities required for the first usable release.

## Non-goals

State what V1 intentionally does not attempt.

## Product rules

Record scoring, permissions, deadlines, visibility, tie handling, and other rules that must be implemented consistently.

## Data and privacy

What data exists, where it is stored, who can read or edit it, how it is backed up, and how it can be deleted or exported?

## Architecture and deployment

Name the chosen stack, hosting, storage, and deployment path, with a short reason for each.

## Visual direction

Summarize the intended personality and link to `design-reference.md`.

## Definition of V1 done

Give observable end-to-end checks that establish the app is ready.

## Owner-only boundaries

List any account, billing, privacy, deployment, or destructive action the factory must escalate.

## Open decisions

Keep unresolved questions here. Do not write implementation tickets that depend on them.
